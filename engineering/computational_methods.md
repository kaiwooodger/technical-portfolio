# Computational Methods

## DICOM-RT and spatial dosimetry

- Relationship-chain and frame-of-reference validation
- Native dose-grid preservation and explicit bounded resampling
- Structure masks, dose-volume endpoints and external TPS evidence
- Vertex identification, nearest-neighbour graphs and edge-local valley sampling
- Spatial PVDR, saddle-path diagnostics, dose gradients and vertex-level QA

## Radiobiological modelling

- Linear-quadratic and modified linear-quadratic response interfaces
- Equivalent uniform dose and tumour-control research summaries
- Reaction–diffusion fields for ROS-like and cytokine-like signalling
- Anatomy-aware vascular uptake through explicit vessel masks
- Model ablation, parameter sensitivity and hypothesis-generating endpoint comparison

The non-local model uses a time-integrated mediator exposure, `H(x)`, and applies a configured survival modifier through a log-survival decrement. These are model-derived quantities, not delivered absorbed dose or clinical toxicity predictions.

## Particle transport and beam analysis

- TOPAS input generation and runtime adapters
- Reproducible random seeds and run manifests
- Energy and quadrupole sweeps
- Integrated depth metrics and one-dimensional percentage depth dose
- Transverse Gaussian widths and asymmetry ratios
- Monte Carlo repeat-run uncertainty and dose-substrate smoothing sensitivity

## Multiscale digital-twin methods

- DICOM-LPS geometry and invertible motion maps
- Closed-loop 0D systemic and pulmonary circulation
- Flow-weighted packet transport and dose history
- Mass-conservative lymph transport
- Radiation-energy mapping and oxygen-response interfaces
- Deterministic event scheduling, random streams and checkpoint/restart
- Gaussian parameter assimilation and uncertainty propagation

## Geometry automation

- Three-dimensional lattice candidate generation
- Target contraction and image-bound predicates
- Minimum-spacing and complete-sphere containment checks
- Atomic structure-set modification and cleanup
- Parameter sweeps across target size, lattice phase, diameter and centre-to-centre spacing

## Statistical and reproducibility methods

- Prespecified endpoint and parameter manifests
- Repeated-seed uncertainty bands
- Sensitivity and falsification analyses
- Descriptive within-case association with small-sample warnings
- Versioned configuration, source and artifact hashes
- Scripted regeneration of publication-facing figures and tables
